# Surface Brief: Landing Page

**Surface:** Landing page (/)
**Mode:** Persuade
**FORM:** seed:2dd0d3ffa45feff5
**Direction:** Nike product campaign landing page

## Commitment

Athletic brand marketing aesthetic with bold hero photography, minimal UI overlays, strong typography, and advanced scroll-triggered animations. Light theme with RallyLoop green accent. Primary action: sign up as a player.

## Key Visual Decisions

- **Hero:** Full-bleed athletic photography (pickleball/badminton/tennis player mid-action) with minimal overlay
- **Typography:** Bold condensed display for headlines, sans-serif for body, RallyLoop wordmark
- **Color:** Black/white with RallyLoop green accent (#39764d) used sparingly for CTAs and highlights
- **Animations:** Scroll-triggered reveals with staggered delays, parallax depth, athletic micro-interactions on buttons
- **Layout:** Hero-first, followed by three asymmetric sections with generous whitespace
- **Fallback:** CSS-based court illustrations with animated elements while photography is commissioned
